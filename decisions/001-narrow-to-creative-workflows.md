# Decision 001: Narrowed the product direction to creative workflows

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Status:** Active

## Context

Sprint 1's goal is to know what I'm building, who it's for, and why it needs to exist. I started
with two broad directions, (1) creative workflows and (2) student school-work-life balance, and
added a third during ideation: (3) operations for student clubs. None of them was a defined
problem yet. To use the rest of Sprint 1 for real discovery, I needed to cut the space down to
one market.

## Options considered

1. **Creative workflows (designers, editors, animators):** pros: I've personally felt every
   pain point on the candidate list (scattered feedback, vague requests, manual brand checks,
   lost assets, learning new software). I can test on myself every week through my design job
   at the BYU Department of Geology and while I learn After Effects. There are more ambitious
   problems here, and AI and agents are central to solving them. Cons: Adobe is shipping AI
   assistants into its apps quickly, and some sub-problems (feedback tools, asset management)
   are already crowded.
2. **Student school-work-life balance:** pros: huge user base, and I'm a user. Cons: the market
   is saturated (Notion, Motion, Todoist, AI planners). As a heavy user of those tools I don't
   see a clear gap where I'd add value.
3. **Student club operations (event runbooks, officer handoff, outreach):** pros: I'm a user
   as PMA VP of Events, users are easy to reach at Marriott, and there's a community-impact
   angle. Cons: the problems felt smaller and less ambitious. It also sits next to Altruvia's
   nonprofit-operations market, which could create a conflict of interest.

## Decision

Narrowed to **creative workflows**. The deciding reason: it has the most ambitious problems
and they're the easiest to test on myself, because I do this work every week.

## What would change our mind

If the manual self-tests and 3–5 interviews per bet (see `discovery/problem-candidates.md`)
show that neither creative bet solves a frequent, painful problem for anyone except me,
reopen club operations (option 3) before Sprint 2.
