# Interview guide: Bet A (AI creative mentor)

**Who to talk to:** anyone who has learned a new creative app (After Effects, Premiere,
Illustrator, Blender, Figma...) in the past 6 months, or is learning one now. Designers who
had to learn something for a specific project are the best fit.

**Length:** 20–30 min. Log each one with `000-interview-template.md`, anonymized.

## What these interviews need to answer

1. **Is the pain real for people other than me?** How often does it happen, and how bad is it?
2. **What do they do today, and what does it cost them?** Time, money, frustration.
3. **Do they want to *learn*, or just want the thing *done*?** This is the biggest risk.
   If most people just want the result, Adobe's in-app assistants already win.
4. **Does learning inside their own project matter,** or are generic tutorials good enough?

## Rules for myself

- Ask about **what they've actually done**, not what they would do. "Tell me about the last
  time..." beats "Would you use...?"
- **Don't pitch until the end.** Once they hear the idea, their answers get polite.
- When they say something vague ("it's kind of annoying"), dig in: "Can you tell me more?"
  or "What happened next?"
- Let silence do the work. Write down their exact words.

## Questions

### 1. Warm-up (2 min)

- What creative apps do you use, and what for? School, work, personal?

### 2. The last time they learned something (10 min)

- Tell me about the last time you had to learn a new app or technique for a project.
  What was the project?
- How did you figure it out? Walk me through it step by step.
  - *If YouTube:* How did you find the right video? How long did it take to find one that
    matched what you needed?
- Where did you get stuck? What did you do when you got stuck?
- How long did it take overall? Is that more or less than you expected?
- Did you finish the project the way you wanted, or settle for something simpler?

### 3. Current tools and spending (5 min)

- Have you ever paid to learn creative software? Courses, Skillshare, LinkedIn Learning,
  Domestika, a tutor? How did that go?
- Have you used AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Adobe's assistant) to help with a creative app? What
  did you ask it? Did it help?
  - *If yes:* Did you want it to do the task for you, or explain how to do it? Why?

### 4. Learn vs. just get it done (5 min)

- When you're stuck in a project, do you usually want to understand how to do it, or just
  get past it? Does that change depending on the situation?
- Think of a skill you learned in a creative app that you use all the time now. How did
  you learn it?
- If an AI could just make the thing for you, would you still want to know how to do it
  yourself? Why or why not?

### 5. Reaction to the idea (5 min, only at the end)

> "Here's what I'm exploring: you describe what you're trying to make, or share a
> screenshot of your project, and an AI mentor teaches you how to build it step by step,
> in your own project instead of a generic tutorial. It can look at your screenshots and
> tell you what's going wrong."

- What's your first reaction?
- When would that have helped you recently? When wouldn't it have?
- What would make you not trust it or stop using it?
- What would you compare it to? (Listen for what they think it replaces.)

### 6. Close (1 min)

- Who else do you know who's learning a creative app right now that I should talk to?
- Can I follow up when I have something for you to try?
