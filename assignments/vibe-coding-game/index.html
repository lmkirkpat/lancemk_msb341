Soft Landing

A timing game about easing curves

How to play

  • A dot flies from the first keyframe to the last. Each round it moves on a different easing curve.
  • Press Space, click, or tap while the dot is inside the amber gate. You get one tap per pass.
  • Closer to the gate's center scores more. Dead center is a Perfect, worth double.
  • Miss the gate or let the dot finish its pass and you lose a life. Three lives.
  • Three hits in a row raise your multiplier, up to ×3.

What makes it different

The gate is a place, not a time. A dot that is easing in to the gate is slow and easy to catch. One that is still accelerating blows past it. After every tap the speed graph opens, the same graph motion designers shape in After Effects, and shades the moments you could have hit. Learn to read the curve and you'll hit more.

Curves you'll meet

  • Linear, Ease In, Ease Out, Easy Ease from round 1.
  • Hold keys from round 4: the dot snaps between poses. Tap while it waits.
  • Overshoot and Bounce from round 6: it can pass the gate twice.
  • Mystery curves from round 9 hide the name for ×1.5 points.
