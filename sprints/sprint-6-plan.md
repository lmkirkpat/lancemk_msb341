# Sprint 6 Plan

**Goal:** Make a wireframe version of my Operation Food Critic app to work on UI/UX design, user
flows, building for mobile app development, and building something that can be tested by a broader
audience. The work lives in the separate Operation Food Critic repo.

**Why this:** Knack got basically sherlocked by Adobe with their newest updates across the creative
suite. Learning to build an AI mentor with a specific scope and focus was a useful exercise, but I
don't see it being viable as an actual product, so I don't want to spend more time on it and want to
explore other new ideas. I've always wanted to try app development, and this project is set up for
me to explore both a new development paradigm and rapid, feedback-based iteration at the same time.

**Done looks like:**

- [ ] A clickable Figma prototype with wireframes for simple app flows that let a user do simple
      onboarding, find a restaurant, log/rate it, and view their "guidebook" log history
- [ ] The prototype submitted for peer testing in the MSB 341 class, and recorded feedback from the
      peer I'm partnered with after they test my "app"
- [ ] A revision round on the prototype based on that feedback
